Open-source electromagnetic simulation, checked against theory

EMCraft models microwave, RF and PCB structures, solves Maxwell's equations with its own finite element and time-domain solvers, and shows S-parameters, fields and far fields. Pure Rust. MIT or Apache-2.0.

Why another field solver

A simulator is only as useful as the trust you can put in its numbers and the freedom you have to look inside it.

Verified

Every solver capability is compared with a closed form, mode matching or a published benchmark. Accepted results are frozen, so a change that moves one fails the build.

Open

The mesher, the finite elements, the direct and iterative solvers and the time domain are all in the repository, each method citing the paper it comes from. No licence server.

What it does

{{samples}} samples open from the start page, their dimensions variables.

  • S-parametersWaveguide, coaxial and planar components; modal, terminal, lumped and Floquet ports; a fast frequency sweep; adaptive refinement.
  • ResonatorsCavity resonances with Q factors, port modes of any cross-section, dispersion diagrams of periodic cells.
  • Antennas and scatteringPerfectly matched layers, plane waves, far fields, directivity and radar cross-section.
  • Time domainFinite integration on hexahedral grids with losses and dispersion, on CPU threads or GPUs.
  • BoardsKiCad boards read whole or drawn in 3D, return-path screening, lumped ports at trace ends.
  • MaterialsDielectric and magnetic losses, Debye, Lorentz and Drude dispersion, walls of finite conductivity.
  • GeometryGuides, boxes, cylinders, spheres and their Booleans; STL, 3MF and SolidCraft designs with exact curved surfaces.
  • StudiesVariables, parameter sweeps and optimization; one project file keeps the model, the mesh and every solve.

Try it

With a Rust toolchain, from a clone of the repository:

cargo run --release -p emcraft -- --sample highpass          # the desktop app, a filter open
cargo run --release -p emcraft-cli -- sample highpass --out pictures   # solve it, compare with the exact solution
