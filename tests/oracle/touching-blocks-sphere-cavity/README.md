# Spherical cavity with touching blocks

The dielectric-loaded spherical cavity (`loaded-sphere-cavity`: radius 10 mm, a concentric
ceramic sphere of εr = 4 and radius 4 mm) with two blocks in the vacuum beside the ceramic,
one standing on the other. The blocks are filled like the vacuum (εr = 1), so the field and the
resonance are the loaded sphere's, known in closed form; what the case checks is the mesh: the
blocks are regions of their own, the smaller one's foot a patch of the larger one's top face that
both regions share triangle for triangle, and the field must cross it as if nothing were there.
A contact left unjoined would be a wall in the cavity and move the resonance by far more than
the tolerance.
