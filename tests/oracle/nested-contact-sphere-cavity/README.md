# Spherical cavity with a disc on the floor of a cylinder

The dielectric-loaded spherical cavity (`loaded-sphere-cavity`) with a small cylinder in the
vacuum beside the ceramic and, inside it, a disc of the cylinder's own radius lying on its floor.
Both are filled like the vacuum (εr = 1), so the resonance is the loaded sphere's, known in closed
form; what the case checks is the mesh. The disc lies against the cylinder's wall from inside —
along its side, where the two are one cylinder divided in two ways, and on its floor, one plane —
so there its surface parts it from the vacuum outside the cylinder, the cylinder's own wall has
a hole, and the three regions must meet node for node around the edges where they come together.
