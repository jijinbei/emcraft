# A quarter of the cavity with a coated sphere

The spherical cavity with a coated sphere (`coated-sphere-cavity`) solved on a quarter: the model
is the same after a quarter turn about z, so only the sector between two planes through the axis
is meshed and the fields on the two cut faces are tied (harmonic m = 0). The three bodies lie on
the axis one inside the next; the sector cuts each of their surfaces once, and each body's part
of the cut faces — a quarter disc, and two quarter rings — is what the traces around it enclose.
TM11's mode about the axis against the three-layer closed form.
